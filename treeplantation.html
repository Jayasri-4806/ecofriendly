PLANT TREES, GROW A BETTER FUTURE

“Every tree we plant today creates a healthier tomorrow.”

CLEAN AIR

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, helping us breathe cleaner air.

PROTECT WATER

Trees help conserve water and protect soil from erosion.

CLIMATE CHANGE

Trees absorb carbon and help keep our planet cooler.

HOW TO PLANT A TREE

01. CHOOSE

Choose a suitable native tree for your local climate and environment.

02. PLANT

Dig a suitable hole and plant the tree carefully in healthy soil.

03. CARE

Water the tree regularly and protect it from damage.

04. GROW

Continue caring for the tree until it becomes strong and healthy.

TREE PLANTATION TIPS

01. Choose the Right Tree

Choose a tree that is suitable for your local climate and soil.

02. Select a Good Location

Plant the tree in a place with enough sunlight and space to grow.

03. Prepare the Soil

Loosen the soil and add compost or organic manure before planting.

04. Water Regularly

Water the plant regularly, especially during hot and dry weather.

05. Protect the Plant

Protect young plants from animals, strong winds and damage.

06. Take Care of the Tree

Remove weeds and provide proper care until the tree grows strong.
